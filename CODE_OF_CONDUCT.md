# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free project community.

Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique ideas and code rather than people, protect private enterprise and user data, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexual, or deliberately disruptive conduct. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior puts people or the project at risk.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Security** tab using *Report a vulnerability*. That form is the only channel in this repository that is private to maintainers by default, so it is used for conduct reports as well. Title the report `Code of Conduct` so it is triaged as a conduct matter rather than a security finding.

Do not open a public issue, and do not include credentials, enterprise data, or personally identifiable information beyond what is necessary to describe the incident.

## Handling

Reports will be reviewed promptly, shared only with people needed to resolve them, and handled without retaliation against good-faith reporters. Maintainers will acknowledge the report and, where appropriate, describe the outcome to the reporter. Enforcement ranges from a private warning through temporary or permanent restriction of participation, depending on severity and pattern. Maintainers who are the subject of a report recuse themselves from reviewing it.
